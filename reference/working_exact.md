# Is the Working Profile of an Equation Its Objective?

`TRUE` where the working response of a distribution parameter's equation
gives the objective exactly: the location of a gaussian family on the
identity link, whose log-likelihood is quadratic in that predictor.
Every other equation reads a quadratic model of its objective.

## Usage

``` r
working_exact(spec, p)
```

## Arguments

- spec:

  A
  [`StatmodSpec()`](https://statmodels7.github.io/statmodels7/reference/StatmodSpec-class.md).

- p:

  The distribution parameter, a string.

## Value

A single logical.
